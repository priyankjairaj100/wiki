\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Temporal memory.}
Graphiti identifies contradictions and closes a fact's interval using a later contradictory fact~\citep{rasmussen2025zep}.
Its implementation includes earliest-time direct invalidation~\citep{graphiti2026source}.
MemStrata applies deterministic supersession to structured keys with one mutable value~\citep{yadav2026memstrata}.
These systems establish direct invalidation and retained history as existing mechanisms.
We analyze their underlying compilation problem when pair decisions need not define consistent groups.
Our results concern all-cutoff equivalence, temporal prefix consistency, and the influence of changed pair decisions.

Re$^3$ extracts and groups facts from retrieved documents~\citep{cao2026re3}.
Its recency filter suppresses conflicting values using publication dates.
It also learns temporal relevance.
Our study holds extraction and relevance fixed while comparing ways to compile replacement decisions.
Reddy and Challaram similarly separate evidence extraction from policy execution~\citep{reddy2026assembly}.
Their analysis motivates comparisons that do not attribute better extraction to a different temporal executor.

\paragraph{Temporal retrieval and reasoning.}
TempRALM combines semantic scores with query--document time distance~\citep{gade2025tempralm}.
Half-life scoring combines semantic relevance with a recency prior~\citep{grofsky2025ragtime}.
These methods select evidence through scores rather than explicit replacement witnesses.
TA-RAG retrieves evidence for diachronic questions~\citep{lau2025tarag}.
IA-RAG represents interval events and uses interval relations for temporal reasoning~\citep{wang2026iarag}.
Its scope includes interval extraction and refinement.
Our certificates instead compile endpoints from supplied pair decisions.
Temporal knowledge graphs also model dated relational evidence~\citep{trivedi2017knowevolve,lacroix2020tensor}.
The relevant distinction here is between direct decisions and closure over inferred claim groups.

\paragraph{Temporal evaluation.}
TimeQA, TempLAMA, and StreamingQA study knowledge that changes over time~\citep{chen2021timeqa,dhingra2022templama,liska2022streamingqa}.
RealTime QA evaluates current questions, while FreshLLMs refreshes evidence through web search~\citep{kasai2023realtimeqa,vu2024freshllms}.
FRESCO includes obsolete passages and current but insufficient passages~\citep{an2026fresco}.
It tests temporal evidence selection beyond global newest-document ranking.
Our experiments add two structural measurements: historical prefix disagreement and the effect of one changed pair decision.
We report retrieval metrics separately from archived reader outputs.

\paragraph{Structured retrieval and conflicting context.}
GraphRAG and HippoRAG use graph structure for evidence organization and retrieval~\citep{edge2024graphrag,gutierrez2024hipporag}.
Their graph structures serve broader retrieval goals than the compiler studied here.
Studies of conflicting context show that answer generation depends on the evidence presented to the reader~\citep{longpre2021conflicts,xie2024conflict}.
Context position also affects language-model behavior~\citep{liu2023lostmiddle}.
These findings support measuring the selected evidence itself.
Our stability bound applies before generation and requires no assumption about reader behavior.
