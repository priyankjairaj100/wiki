\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Temporal memory and replacement.}
Graphiti closes earlier facts through direct contradictory evidence~\citep{rasmussen2025zep,graphiti2026source}.
MemStrata applies deterministic supersession to structured keys~\citep{yadav2026memstrata}.
ERASE edits stored fact histories as new documents arrive~\citep{li2025erase}.
NuggetIndex attaches source spans, validity intervals, and lifecycle states to atomic records~\citep{zerhoudi2026nuggetindex}.
It distinguishes functional and multi-valued relations.
These systems establish direct invalidation and cardinality-aware memory as existing mechanisms.
Our contribution concerns exact compilation across uncertain occurrence dates under fixed direct decisions.

\paragraph{Temporal retrieval.}
Re$^3$ combines fact grouping, conflict filtering, and temporal relevance~\citep{cao2026re3}.
DeFuzzRAG adapts temporal granularity and filters inferred document scopes by query overlap~\citep{chen2026defuzzrag}.
IA-RAG represents intervals and refines uncertain event dates~\citep{wang2026iarag}.
TA-RAG retrieves evidence across timelines~\citep{lau2025tarag}.
These methods motivate interval-aware evidence selection.
We preserve all assignments within supplied occurrence bounds.
The resulting compiler distinguishes possible support from guaranteed support within a window.
The latter uses a different quantifier order from overlap with certainly active points.

\paragraph{Uncertain temporal data.}
Possible and certain temporal semantics have a substantial database foundation.
\citet{anselma2013indeterminacy} study valid-time indeterminacy and compact representations.
\citet{fan2012currency} study data currency without reliable timestamps.
\citet{amarilli2017order} study possible ordered answers and certainty of an entire query result.
Our model restricts uncertainty to independent occurrence bounds with a fixed replacement predicate.
Within this model, two direct witnesses answer all support queries.
The witness bound and local influence result concern this specific compilation problem.

\paragraph{Uncertain ranked answers.}
\citet{hua2008ranking} study top-$k$ membership probabilities under uncertain record membership.
\citet{amarilli2017topk} study unknown ranking values constrained by a partial order.
\citet{feng2023ranking} bound possible and certain results for ranking and SQL window queries.
We keep relevance scores fixed and derive uncertain eligibility from direct temporal replacement.
Within this model, context stability has a linear decision procedure and constructive witnesses.
Possible context membership remains NP-hard when the budget is an input.

\paragraph{Temporal evaluation.}
TimeQA supplies natural passages and questions about dated histories~\citep{chen2021timeqa}.
TempLAMA evaluates factual answers at yearly resolution~\citep{dhingra2022templama}.
StreamingQA and FreshLLMs study changing information access~\citep{liska2022streamingqa,vu2024freshllms}.
FRESCO distinguishes obsolete evidence from current but insufficient passages~\citep{an2026fresco}.
Our protocol preserves complete answer arrays and original answer spans.
It separates snapshot observations from occurrence bounds.
This distinction prevents a benchmark's date labels from silently becoming stronger temporal assumptions.
