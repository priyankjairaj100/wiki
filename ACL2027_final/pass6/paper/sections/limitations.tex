\section*{Limitations}
The context guarantee assumes a fixed relevance ranking and exact support masks.
A ranking that depends on uncertain dates can change even when claim eligibility stays fixed.
Appendix~\ref{app:constrained-dates} gives a concrete counterexample and controlled comparisons.
The fast compiler uses independent date bounds.
The constraint extension enforces known event order and valid lifetimes, including overlapping start and end bounds.
Its exact search can branch exponentially with replacement degree.
The implemented predicates cover overlap, uninterrupted validity, and appointment within a window.
More complex temporal questions need additional predicates.

Source interpretation remains separate from inference over the resulting model.
The automatic adapter models 1.49\% of development units and 1.36\% of test units.
Its recovered cross-claim links occur in calibration articles.
A model-assisted audit accepts 54 of sixty sampled event interpretations.
Eight accepted events omit paragraph-level ends; five additional endpoint interpretations remain uncertain.
Fallback excerpts remain available without certified temporal support.
The separate replacement challenge uses reviewed annotations and boundary queries.
Its twenty paragraphs measure mechanism behavior, rather than representative retrieval accuracy.
Both reviewers are model-assisted, and the recorded source exclusions cannot establish complete absence of earlier exposure.

The answer evaluation uses one small quantized model and sixty primary questions.
Structured decoding controls response syntax without establishing semantic correctness.
The separate eleven-question diagnostic retains a historical cohort selected for certified context disagreement.
TimeQA anchors can be aliases, headings, or mentions outside the relevant period.
Strict annotated-answer metrics differ from expert judgments of semantic correctness.

The evaluated questions have previously inspected outcomes.
Source-audit findings guide the automatic adapter, which remains frozen throughout the reported evaluation.
The earlier TimeQA human and template test tracks overlap.
TempLAMA annual labels can combine concurrent values with within-year succession.
The matched Graphiti comparison isolates its temporal policy kernel under supplied candidates.
It does not evaluate Graphiti's semantic extraction model.
The release preserves source licenses, raw results, and each experiment's scope.
