\begin{abstract}
Sources often date historical changes only to a month or year.
Guessing exact dates can change which evidence reaches an answer model.
We ask when that uncertainty matters to retrieval.
Our compiler stores at most two replacement witnesses per claim.
These witnesses identify evidence that could apply and evidence that must apply somewhere within the requested period.
We give an exact test for whether every allowed timeline selects the same ranked context.
When the test fails, we construct two valid timelines with different contexts.
We also identify support decisions that every stabilizing date refinement must resolve.
These operations take linear time, although general possible-context membership is NP-hard.
A source-only TimeQA evaluation covers 2,348 validation questions.
Among 375 parsed questions with modeled evidence in context, 306 have certified stable contexts.
The remaining 69 expose consequential date ambiguity.
The implementation preserves source spans, explicit departures, and unparsed evidence through answer generation.
\end{abstract}
