# Third-party assets

The release preserves the original license and provenance files for reused assets.

- TimeQA: the authors' BSD 3-Clause license appears in `pass2/naturaldata/raw/TimeQA_LICENSE`.
- Wikipedia-derived text retains its source article identifiers and original wording. Applicable source terms remain in force.
- Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct-GGUF: the Qwen Research License appears in `pass4/inference/provenance/Qwen3B-LICENSE`.
- llama.cpp: the MIT license appears in `pass4/inference/provenance/llama-LICENSE`.
- Graphiti: the original license and extraction notice appear in `pass2/baselines/vendor/`.
- ACL style files retain their license notices within the files.

The archives do not contain Qwen model weights or the llama.cpp runtime binaries.
Pinned asset identifiers and hashes appear in `pass4/inference/provenance/assets.json`.
Model download instructions remain in `pass4/inference/download_assets.py`.

Use the data and code for research reproduction under the original asset terms.
No new license replaces third-party license terms.
