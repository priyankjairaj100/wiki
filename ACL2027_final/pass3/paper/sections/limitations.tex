\section*{Limitations}
The guarantees use independent occurrence bounds and fixed directed replacement decisions.
Dependencies such as a known succession order can restrict the allowed timelines.
The product model then gives conservative support bounds rather than an exact representation of those dependencies.
Incorrect dates or pair decisions can also produce incorrect temporal conclusions.
The compiler executes the supplied model; it does not establish the truth of extracted claims.

The query predicate asks whether evidence applies somewhere within a period.
Questions requiring uninterrupted validity throughout a period need a different predicate.
The compact certificates answer support and stability queries.
Replaying complete contexts retains the accepted graph and uses additional audit state.
The hardness result treats the context budget as an input.
It does not establish hardness for a fixed budget of five.

The rule adapter models 1.46\% of development source units and accepts no cross-claim replacement pairs.
Its natural-data comparison therefore exercises uncertain starts and explicit ends.
The two-witness cross-claim mechanism is verified through proofs, independent execution checks, and separate calibration cases.
Unknown fallback text remains available but has no certified temporal support.
Stable fallback-heavy contexts must not be interpreted as complete temporal understanding.

The new reader study uses a small quantized model and a 24-question pilot.
Its completion-sensitive diagnostic is selected by context disagreement.
Neither establishes gains for larger readers or broad answer-generation workloads.
TimeQA anchors can be aliases, headings, or mentions outside the relevant period.
Strict annotated-answer metrics differ from expert judgments of semantic correctness.
Additional source entailment annotation would improve the empirical assessment.

The earlier TimeQA human and template tracks overlap.
The TempLAMA annual labels can combine concurrent values with within-year succession.
The matched Graphiti comparison isolates its temporal policy kernel under supplied candidates.
It is not a full-system comparison with its semantic extraction model.
The release preserves original source licenses, raw results, and the scope of each experiment.
