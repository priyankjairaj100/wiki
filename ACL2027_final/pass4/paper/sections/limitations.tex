\section*{Limitations}
The guarantees use independent occurrence bounds and fixed directed replacement decisions.
Dependencies such as a known succession order can restrict the allowed timelines.
The product model then provides conservative support bounds for those dependencies.
Incorrect source interpretations can also produce incorrect temporal conclusions.
The certificates establish exact decisions for the supplied model, rather than the truth of extracted claims.

The query predicate asks whether evidence applies somewhere within a period.
Questions requiring uninterrupted validity or a new appointment within that period need different predicates.
Compact certificates answer support and stability queries.
Replaying complete contexts also retains the accepted graph.
The hardness result treats the context budget as an input.
It does not establish hardness for a fixed budget of five.

The adapter models 2.37\% of development units and 2.16\% of test units.
Confirmation primarily exercises uncertain starts and explicit ends.
All recovered cross-claim pairs occur in calibration articles.
An independent model-assisted audit accepts 52 of 60 sampled events.
Eleven accepted starts omit terminations stated elsewhere in their paragraphs.
Unknown fallback text remains available without certified temporal support.
These findings distinguish source interpretation from exact reasoning over its output.

The reader study uses one small quantized model and a fixed 60-question sample.
Its separate eleven-question diagnostic selects contexts with certified disagreement.
TimeQA anchors can be aliases, headings, or mentions outside the relevant period.
Strict annotated-answer metrics differ from expert judgments of semantic correctness.
Larger readers and human source annotations would strengthen the empirical assessment.

The confirmation tracks have previously inspected outcomes.
All new algorithm choices use the declared calibration materials, with code and inputs frozen before confirmation.
The earlier TimeQA human and template test tracks overlap.
TempLAMA annual labels can combine concurrent values with within-year succession.
The matched Graphiti comparison isolates its temporal policy kernel under supplied candidates.
It does not evaluate Graphiti's semantic extraction model.
The release preserves source licenses, raw results, and each experiment's scope.
