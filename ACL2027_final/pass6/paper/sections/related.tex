\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Temporal memory and replacement.}
Graphiti closes earlier facts through direct contradictory evidence~\citep{rasmussen2025zep,graphiti2026source}.
MemStrata applies deterministic supersession to structured keys~\citep{yadav2026memstrata}.
ERASE edits stored fact histories as new documents arrive~\citep{li2025erase}.
NuggetIndex attaches provenance and temporal states to atomic records~\citep{zerhoudi2026nuggetindex}.

\paragraph{Temporal retrieval.}
Re$^3$ combines fact grouping, conflict filtering, and temporal relevance~\citep{cao2026re3}.
DeFuzzRAG adapts granularity and filters document scopes by query overlap~\citep{chen2026defuzzrag}.
IA-RAG represents intervals and refines uncertain event dates~\citep{wang2026iarag}.
TA-RAG retrieves evidence across timelines~\citep{lau2025tarag}.
Our certificates distinguish possible and guaranteed support across every permitted occurrence date.

\paragraph{Uncertain temporal data.}
\citet{anselma2013indeterminacy} study valid-time indeterminacy and compact representations.
\citet{fan2012currency} study data currency without reliable timestamps.
\citet{amarilli2017order} study possible ordered answers and query-result certainty.

\paragraph{Uncertain ranked answers.}
\citet{hua2008ranking} study top-$k$ membership probabilities under uncertain record membership.
\citet{amarilli2017topk} study unknown ranking values constrained by a partial order.
\citet{feng2023ranking} bound ranking results and SQL window queries.
We keep relevance scores fixed and derive uncertain eligibility from direct temporal replacement.

\paragraph{Temporal evaluation.}
TimeQA supplies natural passages and questions about dated histories~\citep{chen2021timeqa}.
TempLAMA evaluates factual answers at yearly resolution~\citep{dhingra2022templama}.
StreamingQA and FreshLLMs study changing information access~\citep{liska2022streamingqa,vu2024freshllms}.
FRESCO distinguishes obsolete evidence from current but insufficient passages~\citep{an2026fresco}.
